# LightBurn Protocol
LightBurn Protocol is an open communication protocol designed to allow software, controllers, firmware, and related tooling to communicate in a more consistent and interoperable way.
The project is released under the MIT License in the hope that manufacturers, developers, and the broader maker ecosystem can build compatible implementations without unnecessary barriers.

## Safety Notice
This protocol may be used in environments involving lasers, CNC systems, motion control hardware, power electronics, and other potentially hazardous equipment.
Anyone implementing or deploying the protocol is solely responsible for ensuring their systems operate safely and comply with all applicable laws, standards, and regulations. This includes validating machine behavior, implementing appropriate emergency stop and interlock systems, enforcing movement and thermal protections, and ensuring the overall safety of the end product.
LightBurn and MillMage Software do not review, certify, or guarantee the safety of third-party implementations unless explicitly stated otherwise.

## Support Expectations
While we hope the protocol encourages broad adoption, we cannot guarantee support for third-party implementations, unofficial firmware, modified protocol variants, or experimental integrations.
Support availability may vary depending on the implementation and the circumstances involved.

## Versioning and Stability
The protocol will evolve over time as the ecosystem grows and implementation experience improves.
Developers should expect specifications to mature gradually and should avoid relying on undocumented behavior or assumptions. Careful testing and validation is strongly encouraged before deployment in production environments.
